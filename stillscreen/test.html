Help test Stillscreen

Stillscreen is a focus app that helps you put your phone down. It is not on the Play Store yet, and I need testers for about two weeks. It takes two minutes. You need an Android phone.

1Join the testers group

Sign in with the Google account your Play Store uses, then tap Join group.

Join the group

2Become a tester

Do this after step 1. On the page that opens, tap “Become a tester”.

Become a tester

3Install the app

On that same page, tap “Download it on Google Play”, then Install.

Please keep the app installed for the whole two weeks and try a focus session once or twice. If anything feels broken, message me with your phone model.

Hinglish mein

Ye ek focus app hai jo abhi Play Store pe nahi aaya hai. Mujhe lagbhag 2 hafte ke liye testers chahiye. Sirf 2 minute lagenge, aur Android phone chahiye.

  1. Wahi Google account use karo jo tumhare Play Store me hai, aur upar “Join the group” dabao.
  2. Uske baad “Become a tester” dabao.
  3. Wahi page pe “Download it on Google Play” dabao aur install karo.

App 2 hafte tak installed rakhna aur ek-do baar focus session try karna. Kuch galat lage to apna phone model bata dena.

Trouble?
  • “Not found” or “not available”: wait a few hours and try again, and check you used the same Google account in steps 1 and 2.
  • “You can’t become a tester”: do step 1 first.
  • Nothing to install: it can take a few hours after you join the group.
