SiteStamp Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

SiteStamp (“the app”) is a camera app that stamps the date, time, GPS location and a note onto photos. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Developer / contact: grvthakur, gaurav954624@gmail.com

Short version

  • No account is needed.
  • Your photos, notes and settings stay on your device.
  • The app has no ads and no analytics, and we do not sell your data.
  • The optional address lookup uses your phone’s own location service.

What the app accesses

PermissionWhy
CameraTo take photos.
Location (while the app is open)To read your GPS position and print it on the photo. The app does not use background location.
Photo library (save only)To save finished photos to a “SiteStamp” album on your device.

What is stored, and where

  • Photos: saved to your device’s gallery. They include the stamp, and the GPS coordinates are also written into the image metadata. The app does not upload them.
  • Notes and settings: your recent notes and your settings (for example the address and grid switches) are stored locally on your device.
  • Temporary files: working copies made while saving a photo are deleted right after the photo is saved.

Address lookup

If the address feature is available and switched on, the app asks your phone’s built-in geocoding service to turn your coordinates into a street address. On most Android phones this service is provided by Google, so your coordinates may be sent to that provider under its own privacy policy. The app itself does not send your location to our servers. You can switch this feature off in the app’s settings.

Sharing

We do not receive, sell or share your photos or location. If you share a photo from your gallery, the stamp and the location data in it go wherever you send it.

Children

SiteStamp is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

  • Turn off the address lookup in the app’s settings.
  • Revoke camera or location permission at any time in your phone’s settings.
  • Delete photos from your gallery at any time. Uninstalling the app removes its notes and settings.

Changes

If the app changes how it handles data (for example by adding accounts, cloud backup, ads or in-app purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
