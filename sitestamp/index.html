SiteStamp Privacy policy

GPS photo proof camera for job sites

SiteStamp prints the date, time, GPS location, address and your own note directly onto every photo, so you can show where and when the work was done.

Coming soon to Google Play

What you get

Timestamp and GPS stamp

Date, time and time zone, plus latitude, longitude, altitude and GPS accuracy printed on the photo.

Address on the photo

The street address of where you stood, looked up from your location. Photos are still stamped with GPS if the lookup is not available.

Your own note

Add a note such as “Before”, “After” or “Site visit”. Recent notes are one tap away.

GPS ready before you shoot

GPS starts when the camera opens. A status pill shows “Acquiring GPS” or “GPS Ready” with the accuracy.

Mock location warning

If the phone reports a fake location, the app flags it on screen and on the photo.

Simple and fast

Tap to focus, pinch to zoom, flash, front and back camera, and optional grid lines. Photos save to a SiteStamp album in your gallery.

How it works

  1. Open SiteStamp and allow camera and location access.
  2. Wait for the green “GPS Ready” pill.
  3. Type an optional note and tap the shutter.
  4. Find the stamped photo in your gallery’s SiteStamp album.

Who it is for

Site inspectors, contractors and technicians, delivery and field sales teams, surveyors, and property and rental inspectors. Anyone who needs a date, time and location stamp on a photo.

Questions

Does it need internet?

Taking photos and stamping GPS coordinates works without internet. The street address lookup needs a connection.

Is a stamped photo tamper-proof?

No. The stamp is a helpful record, but it is not a tamper-proof or legally certified document. GPS accuracy also depends on your phone and surroundings and can be weaker indoors.

Is it free?

The app is free to use. A paid Pro option is planned, with extras such as removing the small “Made with SiteStamp” line and adding the street address.

What data does it collect?

There is no account, no ads and no analytics. Your photos and notes stay on your device. Read the full privacy policy.
