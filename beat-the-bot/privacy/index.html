Beat the Bot – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Beat the Bot ("the app") is an offline brain-training game. This policy explains what information the app uses and what choices you have. The app is published by grvthakur ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

We do not collect your personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. We do not receive your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

2. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves the following on your phone only: your rank and rank points, your daily streak, your best scores, your difficulty and vibration settings, and whether you have finished the welcome screens. This information never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This happens through Google's software in the app, and we do not receive this information.

Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google advertising technologies.

4. Your choices

  • Consent (Europe, UK and similar regions): where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing ads, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. You choose whether to watch one.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help.

6. Security

Your game data stays on your device. Ads are delivered by Google over secure connections.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
