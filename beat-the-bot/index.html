Questions

Is Beat the Bot free?

Yes. The game is free and supported by ads. Rewarded ads, such as earning a streak freeze, are always optional.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All four games work offline. The internet is only used to load ads when you are connected.

What happens to my data?

Your rank, streak and scores stay on your phone, and there are no accounts. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Read the full privacy policy.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones. Check the Google Play listing for the supported versions once the app is live.
