How to make a UPI QR code for your shop

UPI is a payment system used in India. A UPI QR code holds your UPI ID, so customers can pay you by scanning it with their UPI app. This guide shows how to make one with QR & Barcode Toolkit.

Make your code

  1. Open the app and tap Generate, then choose UPI.
  2. Enter your UPI ID, like name@bank. The app tells you if it looks wrong.
  3. Add your Payee name, so customers see who they are paying.
  4. Leave the Amount empty if each customer pays a different amount. Fill it in only for a fixed price.
  5. Add a short Note if you like.
  6. Tap Save PNG or Share.

Before you print it: scan your own code with a UPI app and check that your name and UPI ID are right. A typo in the ID would send payments to someone else.

Check a payment QR before you pay

  • Scan it in the app. It shows who you are paying and the amount before anything opens.
  • Be careful with a code that is a payment request rather than a payment. The app warns you about these. You never need to approve a request to receive money.
  • If the name doesn't match the shop in front of you, don't pay.

Good to know

  • This makes a normal UPI payment code. The app doesn't take or hold any money.
  • Keep your UPI ID private if you don't want it on printed codes.
  • These checks help, but they can't guarantee a code is safe. Use your own judgment.
