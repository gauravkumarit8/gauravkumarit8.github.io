How to scan many QR codes and barcodes and export to CSV

Need to scan a whole box of items, a list of tickets, or a table of codes? Batch mode reads codes one after another into a list that you can share or export to a spreadsheet.

Steps

  1. Open the app on the Scan tab.
  2. Tap the Batch scan button.
  3. Point the camera at each code. Every code you read is added to the list.
  4. When you are done, tap Export CSV. You can also share the list as plain text.
  5. Open the file in any spreadsheet app, such as Excel or Google Sheets.

About CSV export

CSV export is part of Pro. If you are not a Pro user, you can watch one short ad to export once.

Cells that begin with =, +, - or @ get a leading apostrophe, so a spreadsheet never runs scanned text as a formula.

Tips

  • Hold the phone steady and give each code a moment, especially in dim light. Use the flashlight if needed.
  • Everything is saved in your History too, where you can search it and star favorites.
  • Scanning happens on your phone, and you don't need an account.
